Dear family in Christ, peace to you!

The new school year has begun, and many homeschooling families and learning communities have returned to their desks. At this new beginning, let us pray especially for the parents and teachers who guide and walk alongside the next generation.

Moses looked back on Israel's forty years in the wilderness in Deuteronomy:

And thou shalt remember all the way which the LORD thy God led thee these forty years in the wilderness, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments, or no. And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, which thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know; that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD doth man live.
—Deuteronomy 8:2–3 (KJV)

The wilderness journey was not easy, yet it was there that God shaped His people.

Choosing homeschooling or education in a learning community is also a path that requires faith and sacrifice. There is no ready-made framework on which to rely completely. Parents and teachers often have to keep going amid uncertainty, persevere through difficulty, and learn anew to trust God when weary.

We hope that children will gain more than knowledge and good grades. Through their studies, may they also be formed: not giving up easily when facing difficulty, finding the courage to begin again after failure, and learning patience, responsibility, and perseverance through challenges.

This month, please join us in praying especially for them:

  • May God strengthen the faith of parents and teachers, so that they continue to trust Him even when they cannot see the way ahead.
  • May God give them a spirit of endurance and patience, so that they do not avoid difficulty but grow through discipline.
  • May God grant wisdom as they nurture the next generation, helping them know when to comfort, when to encourage, and when to persevere and set expectations.
  • May God help us receive His Word as the bread of life, drawing strength and direction from Him in times of weariness, confusion, and weakness.
  • May God raise up spiritual companions, so that parents and teachers can support, watch over, and pray for one another rather than walk alone.

In this new school year, may we seek not only for God to remove difficulties, but also be willing for Him to shape us and our children through them, so that we may know and trust Him more deeply and cultivate the virtues of prudence, self-control, justice, and perseverance.

Thank you for remembering them in your prayers!

May the Lord Himself strengthen every parent and teacher who is making sacrifices for the next generation, and may they experience His sufficient grace and faithfulness on the road ahead.
