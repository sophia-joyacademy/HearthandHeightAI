Dear family in Christ, grace and peace to you.

Two years ago, I asked a brother who had just completed his Ph.D. in the sciences at a prestigious university why he chose not to continue in research after graduation, but instead entered full-time ministry. His answer deeply impacted me:

“There are many scientists who have faith, but very few pastors with a scientific background. I want to become a pastor with a scientific background so that I can serve this community of professionals.”

Those words have stayed with me ever since.

The Apostle Paul wrote: “I have become all things to all people so that by all possible means I might save some.” 1 Corinthians 9:22

My prayer is that God would raise up a generation firmly rooted in Christ and committed to excellence in their God-given callings. Whether God leads them into science, education, medicine, engineering, business, technology, or the arts, may they become faithful witnesses of Christ and serve as salt and light in every sphere of society.

Across China, many homeschooling families and small learning communities are faithfully investing in the spiritual formation and character development of their children. This is a precious foundation. Yet we also need a larger vision: not only to raise children who love God, but to prepare young men and women who will pursue excellence and glorify God through their work and service in society.

As a new school year begins, please join us in praying for homeschooling families, learning communities, educators, and church leaders who are investing in the next generation.

  • Pray that God would give families and educational leaders clear vision and purpose.
  • Pray that God would raise up faithful, capable, and wise educators.
  • Pray that homes, learning communities, and churches would work together in nurturing the next generation.
  • Pray that children would grow in character, faith, wisdom, and academic excellence.
  • Pray that they would one day serve Christ faithfully in every profession and every field to which God calls them.

“You are the salt of the earth... You are the light of the world.” Matthew 5:13-14

Thank you for remembering these families, educators, and future leaders in your prayers.
